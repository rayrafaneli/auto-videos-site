Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

What it is

auto-videos is a personal tool that publishes short videos to its author's own YouTube channels (Refúgio da Alma, QI Supremo and Fato Oculto). It uses the YouTube API Services. By using this tool you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and data handled through the API is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Data we access

  • The authorized channel's name and ID (to make sure videos go to the right channel).
  • Videos uploaded by the tool itself and their status (private, scheduled, public), in order to schedule, publish or delete those videos.

We do not access, collect or store viewer data, comments, subscribers, messages or any information about other users.

How we use and store it

Data is used solely to publish and manage videos on the author's channels. The authorization token is stored privately (local file and encrypted GitHub Actions secrets) and is never displayed, sold or shared with third parties. We use no cookies or tracking.

Sharing

No data is shared with third parties. Videos are only sent to YouTube.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google Account permissions page: myaccount.google.com/permissions. Once revoked, the tool can no longer publish, and stored authorization data is deleted within 7 days, or immediately upon request through the contact below.
